# Privacy policy review before publication

The website policy is an international baseline tailored to the current repository, not a certification of worldwide legal compliance. It covers website enquiries, not all apps or client products.

Before publishing, the business owner should confirm:

- The full legal identity, business address, country of establishment, and any required privacy representative or data protection officer; add those details to section 1.
- That contact@saz-labs.com is monitored for rights requests.
- Actual hosting and email providers, Firebase processing locations, subprocessors, and applicable international transfer safeguards; make section 6 specific to the verified arrangements.
- Actual retention and deletion practices, including backups and provider logs. Section 7 gives retention criteria, not an implemented deletion schedule.
- A working procedure to access, correct, export, and delete enquiry records. Current Firestore client rules prohibit updates and deletions; authorised administrative tooling is needed to fulfil these requests. The policy addition does not change those rules.
- Whether business-wide sales, sharing, analytics, marketing, or sensitive-data processing occurs outside this repository, and whether California or other regional supplemental disclosures and request methods are required. The website's current code cannot establish historical practices for the prior 12 months.
- Whether any local consent requirements apply to third-party font requests or browser storage. This change does not install a consent manager or claim that one exists.

Review the notice when adding providers, analytics, advertising, payments, accounts, or new products. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review for the markets served; a single generic policy cannot guarantee compliance everywhere.
